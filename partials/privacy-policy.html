Privacy policy

This policy describes what GamersHUB stores, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Information we collect

When you register, we collect account details such as your display name, username, email address, password hash, profile preferences, and content you choose to publish. We also record session, device, and security information needed to protect accounts and keep the service working.

How we use information

We use this information to provide profiles, messaging, feeds, authentication, email verification, account security, and requested notifications. We may use aggregated technical information to diagnose errors and improve the application.

Sharing and storage

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, databases, email delivery, or application operation. We retain information while your account is active or as needed for security and legitimate project records.

Your choices

You can review and update available profile and privacy settings through your account. You may request account information or deletion from the project administrators, subject to security checks and records we must retain.

Security

Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and authenticated actions use session and request protections. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a unique password and report suspicious activity promptly.

Policy changes

We may revise this policy as GamersHUB develops. The latest version and its effective date will always be available on this page.
